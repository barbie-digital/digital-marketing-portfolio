
# Social Media Marketing Content Calendar

## Project Overview
This is a beginner-level social media marketing project created for a fictional online clothing store named TrendyWear.

## Marketing Objective
- Increase brand awareness.
- Improve Instagram engagement.
- Attract potential customers.
- Promote new clothing collections.

## Target Audience
- Age group: 18–30 years
- People interested in fashion and online shopping.
- Instagram users.

## 7-Day Content Calendar

| Day | Content Type | Content Idea | Objective |
|---|---|---|---|
| Monday | Instagram Post | Introduce the brand | Brand awareness |
| Tuesday | Reel | Trending outfit ideas | Reach |
| Wednesday | Story | Fashion poll | Engagement |
| Thursday | Carousel | 5 styling tips | Education |
| Friday | Reel | New collection showcase | Product awareness |
| Saturday | Story | Customer feedback | Trust |
| Sunday | Post | Weekly offers | Sales promotion |

## Sample Instagram Caption

"Upgrade your style with TrendyWear! ✨
Discover outfits that match your personality.
Shop your favourite looks today!"

## Hashtags
#TrendyWear #FashionStyle #OnlineShopping #FashionTrends #StyleInspiration

## Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)
- Reach
- Impressions
- Likes
- Comments
- Shares
- Saves
- Profile visits

## Expected Outcome
This content calendar aims to improve brand awareness, audience engagement and product visibility.
